## One-line thesis

An established bullish impulse pulled back into a repeated structural support zone. The first attempt was followed by another test, and the `2025-06-02` H2 signal K was confirmed when `2025-06-03` broke above its high. The setup's quality came from confluence and available space, not from the H2 label alone.

## Market structure

### A leg: initial bullish impulse

From `2025-05-07` to `2025-05-14`, price advanced from approximately `67.45` to `79.79`. The move had clear directional pressure and created the background for a continuation pullback study.

### First attempt: H1 research signal

On `2025-05-23`, price tested the EMA20 area and the left-side support region. The bar showed a rejection of lower prices and recovered toward/above the EMA20. This is a plausible H1 research signal, but the first attempt did not immediately produce sustained expansion.

### Second attempt: H2 research signal

On `2025-05-30`, price made a deeper test of the same broad support region. The move approached the user's measured 50% retracement reference and remained near the repeated low cluster. On `2025-06-02`, price recovered and held above the EMA20 area. This is the clearer H2 signal K for the study.

### Confirmation

The research entry confirmation is a buy stop just above the `2025-06-02` signal K high. The `2025-06-03` move through that high is confirmation; merely observing the H2 signal K is not an entry.

## META confluence

The case contains the following aligned advantages:

1. Established bullish context;
2. Clear prior impulse;
3. Repeated lows in a common support area;
4. First test near EMA20;
5. Deeper second test near the measured 50% retracement;
6. Recovery back above EMA20;
7. H2 second attempt;
8. Buy-stop confirmation above the signal K;
9. Measured-move space toward the upside;
10. A potentially constructive pullback-volume observation, treated as a non-required reference.

The 50% retracement, EMA test, repeated lows, and H2 label should not be counted as independent advantages when they describe the same price zone. META improves the quality of the location hypothesis; it does not guarantee a higher realized win rate.

## Entry, stop, and target geometry

### Entry

Research entry: place a buy stop just above the high of the `2025-06-02` H2 signal K. The exact price must be calculated from the actual bar and tick size before any implementation.

### Structural stop

The study stop is approximately `72.00`, below the main repeated-support zone. A stop just below the `2025-05-30` low or the `2025-06-02` low may still sit inside the support band and can be vulnerable to a normal retest.

This is a case-specific research level, not a universal stop rule. The live or backtest stop must be recalculated from the instrument, timeframe, volatility, and actual entry.

### Space and targets

- First independent obstacle: identify before entry from prior highs, range boundaries, or other major structure.
- Minimum candidate geometry: at least `1R` to the first meaningful obstacle.
- Complete swing preference: approximately `2R` or better.
- The user's broader Measured Move projection was near `87.00`, which provided sufficient historical space relative to a `72.00` structural stop in the study geometry.
- A Measured Move is a target area. If price approaches the target while momentum weakens, partial profit-taking is reasonable before the exact projection.

## Volume and momentum

The user's Wyckoff observation is that a pullback with contracting volume can indicate reduced selling pressure in an intact uptrend. This is a useful confluence observation, not a required condition. Volume must be interpreted with price response, location, and follow-through; low volume alone does not authorize an entry.

The later move from `2025-06-03` to approximately `2025-06-17` showed stronger continuation characteristics, including gaps and directional closes. After approximately `2025-06-18` and `2025-06-20`, smaller bodies and more overlap suggested a mature or slowing advance. That change supports staged profit management rather than insisting on an exact target print.

## Sector and event filters

- Check SOXX or the relevant semiconductor-sector context before treating a KLAC long as aligned.
- Prefer sector alignment; a strong single-stock setup against a weak sector is a counter-market exception and should not be treated as ordinary permission.
- Do not open a new position within the three trading sessions before scheduled earnings.
- Event-driven gaps must be separated from ordinary Price Action gaps. Record the event date before interpreting the gap as support, resistance, or a measured-move magnet.

## Invalidation and management states

### Working

- The support zone holds;
- price accepts above the H2 trigger;
- follow-through appears within a reasonable number of bars;
- sector context does not materially contradict the long thesis.

### Warning

- price repeatedly returns to the trigger;
- follow-through weakens;
- the advance becomes overlapping and climactic;
- sector strength fades;
- price reaches the target zone while momentum contracts.

Warning is a reason to manage or reduce risk according to a predefined plan, not an automatic reversal signal.

### Invalid

- price accepts below the structural support zone;
- the stop is hit;
- the breakout trigger fails and price returns through the area the H2 thesis required it to leave;
- a material event changes the information set before confirmation.

## What this case teaches

1. H2 is stronger when the second attempt occurs at a real structural zone, not merely near an EMA.
2. A signal K and an entry confirmation are separate events.
3. A structural stop can be wider than the latest local low while still offering acceptable space.
4. A 50% retracement is a useful reference, but its value comes from confluence.
5. Parent legs and nested legs can coexist; leg segmentation should be revisited as more bars develop.
6. Measured Move targets should be managed as zones, with partial profit-taking when momentum fades.
7. Volume contraction is a non-required confluence item.
8. Sector alignment and earnings avoidance are part of the setup context, not afterthoughts.
